    Before you file
    • File by May 15, or 30 days after your appraisal notice was delivered, whichever is later (Texas Tax Code §41.44). You can file online through your appraisal district.
    • Ask the appraisal district for a copy of the evidence it plans to use at your hearing (Texas Tax Code §41.461). It must make that evidence available to you at least 14 days before the hearing.
    • Bring this letter and your attachments to the hearing; you can also present them at an informal review first.
